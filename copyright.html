3. Privacy and Information Handling

Information you provide

Depending on the site or feature, you may choose to submit a name, email address, message, nickname, game identifier, team or group information, phone number, ratings, votes, or other participation details. Some applications use browser storage or session identifiers to keep a session working. Different projects collect different information; not every category applies to every site.

How information is used

Information is used as needed to respond to messages, provide requested features, administer events and games, manage participation, troubleshoot problems, and maintain the sites. Information is not intended to be sold or used for unrelated advertising.

Services, access, and visibility

Depending on the project, information may be processed or stored using third-party infrastructure such as Supabase, Google services, or the website hosting provider. Administrators may access submissions needed to operate a feature, and certain nicknames, votes, scores, offers, or other participation details may be visible to other users where the feature calls for it. Third-party services operate under their own policies.

Retention and requests

Records may remain until deleted, reset, or no longer needed for the relevant feature. Resetting a game or clearing a browser does not necessarily erase server records, emails, or backups. To ask about personal information or request correction or removal, please use the contact page. Requests will be reviewed subject to technical and legal limitations.

Security

Reasonable care is taken in operating these projects, but no online service is completely secure. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, or other highly sensitive information through general forms or game fields.

4. Web Design Inquiry Privacy

This section applies specifically to the Austin Whyte Web Design project inquiry system when enabled.

Information collected

Contact details and project preferences you choose to submit, along with submission time and a reference ID. Please do not submit passwords, payment information or sensitive personal details.

How information is used and stored

Information is used to respond to your inquiry, assess project requirements and prepare a proposal. Records are stored in a private Google database. Authorized administrators may access them for these purposes. The public form cannot read existing submissions.

Third-party services

This website uses GitHub Pages and Google Fonts. These providers may receive ordinary connection information.

Retention and removal

Information is retained only as needed for inquiry and business administration. Use the 12one contact page to ask about your inquiry or request correction or removal.

5. Availability and Changes

Projects may change, pause, or be discontinued without notice. Features are provided as available, without a guarantee of uninterrupted access. This page may be updated to reflect changes to the collection of sites and services; the update date above indicates the current revision.

Voluntary support

Where offered, voluntary contributions help support development but do not purchase ownership, special access, or permission to reuse protected materials unless expressly stated otherwise.

For copyright, permissions, privacy, accessibility, or general questions, please contact Austin Whyte.
