Meet your designer

Hi, I'm Austin.

Website designer, digital creator, and business student.

I began designing websites in 2020 when I discovered that websites could be used to share important content outside of social media in a visually appealing and engaging way. What started as an interest in website creation has grown into a passion for sharing information, building community, and simplifying everyday processes through technology.

My Experience

Since 2020, I have worked on a variety of website and digital design projects, including:

  • Landing pages for small businesses
  • Websites for college communities
  • Web-based hall community games and activities
  • Interactive quiz and competition platforms
  • Digital event platforms
  • Online scorekeeping and organizational tools
  • Personal websites

These projects have given me opportunities to explore different approaches to website design, visual communication, and practical technology.

Technical Experience

I have experience working with a variety of website design, graphic design, and productivity platforms, including Webnode, Google Sites, HTML, GitHub Pages, Microsoft Office (particularly Excel and PowerPoint), Canva, and more.

My technical experience allows me to approach website design from both a creative and practical perspective.

Why Website Design?

I enjoy taking information and ideas and presenting them in a visually appealing, engaging, and accessible way. Much of my experience has grown out of opportunities to build community within college residence halls, facilitate events, and simplify processes through technology.

Through Austin Whyte Web Design, I want to extend that experience to individuals, small businesses, churches, and organizations looking to establish or improve their online presence.

I offer straightforward pricing, with website design at $40 per hour and a one-time $50 initial setup fee.
