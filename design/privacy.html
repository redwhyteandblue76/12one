Inquiry privacy

This notice describes the project inquiry system when enabled.

Information collected

Contact details and project preferences you choose to submit, along with submission time and a reference ID. Please do not submit passwords, payment information or sensitive personal details.

How it is used

Information is used to respond to your inquiry, assess project requirements and prepare a proposal. Records are stored in a private Google spreadsheet using Google Apps Script. Authorized administrators may access them for these purposes. The public form cannot read existing submissions.

Third-party services

The website uses GitHub Pages and Google Fonts, plus a CDN-hosted QR library on the complimentary QR generator page. QR generation runs locally in the browser; entered URLs are not sent to a QR generation service. These providers may receive ordinary connection information.

Questions and removal

Use the 12one contact page to ask about your inquiry or request correction or removal. Records should be retained only as needed for inquiry and business administration.
