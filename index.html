The Mission:

To worship the Lord in everything I do and lead others in full surrender to Him.

- Romans 12:1

That's my mission. Right there. I hope yours is similar. Let me ask you, when you pulled up this webpage, did you do it with the Lord? When you got up this morning, did you offer the day to Him? When you made that huge decision last month, did you inquire of the Lord and seek His Word for wisdom to follow His leading? When you breathed in and out about three times since starting to read this paragraph, did you take those breaths for the glory of God?

I believe it is possible, and God's desire, for me to live every moment, yes, every moment, for the Lord.

The Vision:

To be so fully devoted to God that every thought, word, and action reflects His heart for those around me, so that the gospel will be preached continuously through the way I conduct my life.

- Ephesians 3:17-4:24

How does this mission culminate? In a life that truly represents Christ. That is the ultimate vision, for the glory of God, to bring others into His Kingdom.

The Values:

01

The Whole Word of God

Recognizing Scripture as the foundation of truth in my life and for the world, and being committed to living under its authority

2 Timothy 3:16-17

02

Glorious Grace from God

Remembering who I am because of God’s grace through Jesus Christ and freely receiving it daily, because I need it

Ephesians 1:4-8

03

Foundational Faith in God

Never losing sight of the gospel through the work of Jesus Christ as the foundation of my faith and relationship with God

1 Corinthians 15:3-5

04

Purposeful Praise to God

Committing myself to intentionally declare God’s greatness through prayer and song

Psalm 34:1

05

Constant Communication with God

Continuously talking to the Lord throughout each day and being sensitive to the Holy Spirit’s guidance

1 Thessalonians 5:17

06

Committed Community through God

Being actively engaged in the local church and living in unity with other believers through the power of the Holy Spirit

Hebrews 10:24-25

07

Worthy Work for God

Working at every position, job, and task with all my heart, offering it as an act of worship

Colossians 3:17
